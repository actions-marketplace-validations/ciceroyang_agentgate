Only Community and the free review below are available now. Team and Enterprise are planned scope, not products available for purchase today. SSO/SAML, RBAC, multi-tenancy, and signed audit exports have not been implemented. SARIF export is available.

Why price by organization rather than seat: the intended deliverable is one record across repositories and teams. Seat-based pricing would discourage broader governance.
